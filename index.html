Cooked the way we eat at home

We started this small counter because our neighbours wanted a clean place for a proper South Indian breakfast without going far. A few years on, the same families still walk in every morning.

Our vada batter is ground fresh, the sambar is made daily, and the misal gets its heat from a masala we blend ourselves. If the plate isn’t good enough for our own family, it doesn’t go out.

Come with your kids, your parents or your office friends. We’ll keep the chai hot.
